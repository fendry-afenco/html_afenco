Privacy Policy

Afen LLC · Effective October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Afen LLC (“Afen,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and shares personal information when you visit afen-co.com or contact us.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us.

  • Contact form: your name, email address, and message.
  • Email updates: your choice to receive occasional insights, if you tick the box on the form.
  • Scheduling: if you book a call through our scheduling tool, your name, email address, and the time you choose.
  • Payments: if you pay for a service, a payment provider processes your payment. We do not receive or store your full card number.

Information collected automatically. Our web host and form service may log technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested. This site does not set its own cookies and does not use advertising or tracking scripts.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to your messages and requests.
  • To schedule, deliver, and follow up on calls and services.
  • To send updates you asked for. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam and abuse.
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

3. How we share information

We share information only with service providers that help us run Afen, and only as needed for their service: our web host, our form-handling service, our scheduling tool, our email provider, and our payment provider.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a sale or restructuring of the business. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies

This site does not set cookies. Third-party services you reach through it, such as a scheduling or payment page, may set their own, and their policies apply there.

5. Email communications

If you ask for email updates, you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any message or by contacting us. We may still send messages about a call or service you requested.

6. How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as needed to respond to you, provide our services, and meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements. We then delete it or make it anonymous.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please do not send passwords, access credentials, or other highly sensitive information through the contact form.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. If you live in California, you have additional rights under California privacy law, including the right to know, delete, and correct your information and to be free from discrimination for exercising those rights. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before we act on it.

9. Children

Our website and services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Links to other sites

Our site links to other websites, such as LinkedIn. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
